# Maplewood seed data

Generated by `scripts/gen-seed.mjs` with a fixed seed, so it's identical on every run. Validate it with `node scripts/validate.mjs`. Maplewood Software Inc. is fictional, and so are its customers and registration numbers. Rates, thresholds and deadline rules are real, verified on official pages on 2026-10-16. For demonstration, not tax advice.

Expected result: 198 of 200 invoice lines and 6 of 7 rates are valid. The agent must report the rejections and must not repair them.

## Conventions the ontology leaves open

- **Supplier:** `Supply` has no supplier property. Every supply in this file is Maplewood's.
- **Currency:** `amount` is in the place of supply's currency: CAD for CA-*, USD for US-*. `Supply` has no currency property.
- **US tax:** the state portion only. Local and MCTD add-ons are out of scope.
- **Quebec:** Maplewood is registered under the **specified QST system**. It charges QST only to consumers (`recipientIsRegistrant: false`). Lines to QST-registered businesses carry GST only.
- **Ontario:** HST at 13% replaces GST, so Ontario lines carry HST only.
